# Technical & Fundamental Confluence Framework

This guide explains how to synthesize fundamental analysis (business quality, valuation) with technical analysis (market trend, price action, momentum) into actionable investment and trading decisions.

---

## 1. Why Confluence Matters

* **Fundamentals without Technicals**: Leads to the "Value Trap" — buying a cheap stock that continues falling for years because market sentiment, institutional liquidation, or hidden structural headwinds remain unaddressed.
* **Technicals without Fundamentals**: Leads to chasing speculative momentum — buying over-hyped rallies that collapse abruptly upon earnings misses or dilution events.
* **Confluence (The Intersection)**: Buying fundamentally strong businesses when the market trend aligns in your favor, or identifying high-probability turning points where fundamental valuation creates a hard floor.

---

## 2. The 4-Quadrant Decision Matrix

```
                      Fundamental Quality & Valuation
                        Weak / Expensive          Strong / Fair-to-Cheap
                    +------------------------+------------------------+
                    |                        |                        |
       Bullish      |      CATEGORY B        |      CATEGORY A        |
       Uptrend      |   Speculative Rally    |   High-Probability     |
                    |   (Short-term Trading) |      Compounder        |
                    |                        |                        |
Technical           +------------------------+------------------------+
State               |                        |                        |
       Bearish      |      CATEGORY D        |      CATEGORY C        |
       Downtrend    |      Value Trap /      |  Value Watchlist /     |
                    |      Capitulation      |  Wait for Stage 1 Base |
                    |                        |                        |
                    +------------------------+------------------------+
```

### Category A: High-Probability Compounder (Institutional Accumulation)
* **Characteristics**:
  * Fundamentals: High ROE (>15%), double-digit revenue/earnings growth, positive FCF, manageable debt.
  * Technicals: Price above rising 50 and 200 SMAs; Higher Highs and Higher Lows; RSI holding 40-70 zone.
* **Action Strategy**:
  * Primary accumulation zone: Buy orderly pullbacks to the 20-day EMA or 50-day SMA.
  * Risk Management: Trail stops below recent higher swing lows or the 50-day SMA.

### Category B: Speculative / High-Beta Rally
* **Characteristics**:
  * Fundamentals: Low or negative margins, high debt, speculative story or high valuation multiple (P/S > 15x).
  * Technicals: Strong momentum, RSI > 70, breaking out on high volume.
* **Action Strategy**:
  * Strictly swing trade / momentum play. Never "buy and hold" or average down.
  * Set tight trailing stop-losses ($2 \times \text{ATR}$ or break of EMA 9/21).

### Category C: Value Opportunity on Watchlist (Accumulation Phase)
* **Characteristics**:
  * Fundamentals: High-quality business, resilient balance sheet, historically low valuation multiples.
  * Technicals: Downtrend or consolidating below descending 200 SMA; market hasn't turned yet.
* **Action Strategy**:
  * **Do not catch a falling knife**.
  * Wait for Stan Weinstein's "Stage 1 Base" (price stops making lower lows and begins moving sideways with volume drying up).
  * Trigger entry when price reclaims the 50-day SMA and breaks out of the consolidation range on volume.

### Category D: Capitulation / Avoid
* **Characteristics**:
  * Fundamentals: Deteriorating margins, declining sales, high debt/EBITDA, negative FCF.
  * Technicals: Death cross active; price below falling 50 and 200 SMAs; RSI failing to break above 50.
* **Action Strategy**:
  * Avoid long positions; consider protective put options or hedging if held in portfolio.

## 3. Position Sizing & Risk Management Rules

1. **Risk per Trade**: Risk no more than 1% to 2% of total portfolio equity on any single position.
2. **Invalidation Level**: Identify the exact price level where your thesis is proven wrong *before* entering (e.g., break of key support, loss of 50 SMA).
3. **Risk-to-Reward Ratio**: Minimum target R:R of $1:2.5$.
   $$\text{R:R} = \frac{\text{Target Price} - \text{Entry Price}}{\text{Entry Price} - \text{Stop Loss}}$$
4. **Earnings Rule**: Avoid initiating full swing positions within 48 hours of an earnings announcement unless explicitly intended as an earnings volatility strategy.
